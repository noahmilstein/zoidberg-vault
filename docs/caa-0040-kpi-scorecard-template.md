# CAA-0040 — KPI Scorecard Template (Weekly)

## Lanes
1. B2C Rentals
2. B2B Rentals
3. B2C Sales+Install
4. B2B Sales+Install

## Core Metrics (per lane)
- Leads
- Qualified leads
- Qualification rate (%)
- Consults booked
- Show rate (%)
- Proposals sent
- Close rate (%)
- Revenue ($)
- Gross margin ($ and %)
- CAC ($)
- Payback period (weeks)

## Rentals-only Metrics
- Fleet utilization (%)
- Add-on attach rate (%)
- Repeat booking rate (%)
- Average booking value ($)

## Sales+Install-only Metrics
- Avg deal size ($)
- Sales cycle length (days)
- Win/loss reason distribution

## Attribution Minimums
- firstTouch: source, medium, campaign, content, term, timestamp
- lastTouch: source, medium, campaign, content, term, timestamp
- campaign_id required for paid channels

## Weekly Review Cadence
- Monday: prior week scorecard review + lane ranking
- Tuesday: messaging/offer iteration decisions
- Wednesday: channel ops checks (ad accounts, outreach deliverability, partner pipeline)
- Friday: capacity and margin check for next week

## Decision Rules
- Scale if: margin positive + payback within target + stable lead quality
- Hold if: margin positive but volatile quality or delivery constraints
- Reduce if: poor quality + negative margin trend over 2 consecutive weeks
